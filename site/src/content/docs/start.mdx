BrainlessLab is an experimental Python and Quadrants platform for studying neural
reservoirs in declared tasks. Begin with one complete record before designing a study.

## Run and inspect one simulation

[Run your first simulation](/tutorials/first-simulation/) walks through installation,
a small delayed-cue run and inspection of its saved result. You should finish with a
complete record, verified checksums and a task outcome in its native units.

That confirms software execution. It does not establish cognition, learning or
biological fidelity.

## Choose your next question

- [Create a reproducible profile](/tutorials/reproducible-profile/) to measure one design across independent trials.
- [Compare conditions](/tutorials/compare-conditions/) to test a declared change using matched worlds.

Read the [system map](/handbook/system-map/) when you want to understand how a plan
becomes a simulation and a saved result.
