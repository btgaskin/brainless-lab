---
title: Experiments and evidence
description: Separate software conformance, development and sealed confirmation.
---

Follow the evidence ladder:

```text
conformance -> calibration -> exploration -> tuning/training -> variance pilot
  -> frozen protocol -> sealed confirmation -> robustness -> promoted evidence
```

A `Plan` retains a question, version, named conditions and evidence state.
The active development runner cannot confer confirmed or promoted evidence.
A successful software gate or complete record does not promote a study.

```bash
uv run brainlesslab check python/plans/delayed-cue.toml
```

The check command verifies a declared protocol without examining or executing a sealed
evaluation set. Keep calibration, tuning, confirmation and robustness seeds disjoint.

A task score operationalises performance on that task. A diagnostic decoder measures
recoverable labelled information under its estimator and split policy.
Neither establishes cognition, general capability or biological fidelity.

Accepted historical contributions, experiment bundles and benchmark tables retain their
source references and status. This rewrite does not rerun, supersede or promote them.
The historical Julia `ExperimentSpec` authoring workflow remains at
[d0fc756d](https://github.com/btgaskin/brainless-lab/tree/d0fc756d).
