Evaluation defines the independent observations for a question.
`EvaluationSpec` declares blocks, trials per block, horizon, warm-up, construction scope,
root seed and scientific seed partition.

```bash
uv run brainlesslab check python/plans/delayed-cue.toml
```

Resolve before execution. Respect task-specific minimum scored horizons and completed
episode requirements. A short diagnostic must remain labelled.

Paired conditions can share declared world streams within a block while keeping their
mechanism streams distinct. Preserve condition, block, trial and entity identities
independently of batch slots. Keep calibration, tuning and confirmation seed banks disjoint.

For offline decoding, construct one fixed wiring per block and fully reset every trial.
Require distinct world seeds across trials. Fit, validation and evaluation splits contain
whole trials, and the same split applies across observation points.

Reset, initial construction and snapshot ownership are software contracts.
They do not establish empirical learning. Continue with
[decoding](/benchmarks/probes/decoding/) and [evidence](/handbook/experiments-evidence/).
