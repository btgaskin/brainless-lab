## Which combinations are available?

The active models are `falandays` and `sorn`. Both accept the receptor and effector
widths declared by the task. They use different local equations and plasticity;
selecting the same node count does not equate their mechanism or memory footprint.

| Task | Receptors | Effectors | Neural frames per world tick | Primary outcome |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Tracking | 62 | 2 | 1 | `track_score` |
| Pong | 46 | 2 | 1 | `hit_rate` |
| Plank CartPole Easy | 8 | 2 | 24 | `fitness` |
| Delayed cue | 3 | 2 | 1 | `recall_accuracy` |
| Recall interference | 3 | 2 | 1 | `probe_accuracy` |
| Delayed XOR | 5 | 2 | 1 | `probe_accuracy` |
| Evidence accumulation | 3 | 2 | 1 | `probe_accuracy` |
| Context integration | 7 | 2 | 1 | `probe_accuracy` |
| Temporal order | 4 | 2 | 1 | `probe_accuracy` |
| Reversal adaptation | 8 | 2 | 1 | `adaptation_accuracy` |

```python
from brainlesslab import TaskSpec
from brainlesslab.tasks import capacity_probe_presets, definition

for kind in ("tracking", "pong", "cartpole_plank_easy", "delayed_cue"):
    task = definition(TaskSpec(kind))
    print(kind, task.n_inputs, task.n_effectors, task.neural_frames)
print(len(capacity_probe_presets()))
```

The final line is `64`. This count includes five delayed-cue cells and six
additional families; it is not 64 independent replications.

## Choose the scoring window

Tracking requires at least 2,000 scored ticks and defaults to 2,000. Pong requires
at least 6,000 and defaults to 7,200. CartPole uses a 15,000-tick maximum and may
terminate earlier. Physical diagnostic runs can be shorter when explicitly marked
diagnostic; they do not satisfy the ordinary task protocol.

Probe horizons follow the complete episode schedule. Increasing a delay changes the
horizon. A run cannot truncate an episode and still claim its declared accuracy.
Probe warm-up is zero.

Task upper bounds are 1 for Tracking, Pong and binary probes, and 15,000 for
CartPole. A bound does not imply an implemented controller achieves it.
Physical null adjustment requires a matching frozen empirical calibration;
binary probes declare chance 0.5.
