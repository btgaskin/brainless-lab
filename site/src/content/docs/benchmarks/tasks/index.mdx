title: Benchmark tasks
description: The four frozen direct-control harnesses and the profile coordinates they measure.
---

A benchmark task is more than an environment name. It fixes the observations, encoder,
actions, timing, termination rule, public profile coordinate, formal scalar outcome, and
score anchors used by every model.

| Task | Public profile coordinate | Better | Formal outcome retained in records |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Tracking](/benchmarks/tasks/tracking/) | mean heading error (degrees) | lower | `track_score` |
| [Pong](/benchmarks/tasks/pong/) | mean longest rally (returns) | higher | `hit_rate` |
| [Plank CartPole Easy](/benchmarks/tasks/cartpole-plank-easy/) | mean balanced steps | higher | `fitness` |
| [Delayed cue](/benchmarks/tasks/delayed-cue/) | recall accuracy | higher | `recall_accuracy` |

These coordinates are part of the declared task protocols for this benchmark. Other
recorded measures remain diagnostics. The formal task outcome still appears with its key,
raw value, and normalised value, but it is not substituted for the public profile
coordinate. The benchmark does not average across tasks.

Wall remains an available task, but it is not a coordinate of this benchmark.
