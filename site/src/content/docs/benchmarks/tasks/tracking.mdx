## What must the controller do?

Tracking moves an observer around a circle to follow a stimulus. The world stores
observer angle `theta` and stimulus angle `phi` in radians. The stimulus moves
one degree per world tick by default and reverses direction every 720 ticks.

Two eyes have default offsets of ±30 degrees. Each eye samples 31 directions from
−60 to +60 degrees in four-degree increments, giving 62 receptor channels.
Each receptor has a four-degree central response plateau and a Gaussian fall-off
outside it. `sensory_gain` scales this signal.

The two effectors move the observer by `movement_amp * (left - right)` degrees
per tick; the default amplitude is 10. There is one neural frame per world tick.

```python
from brainlesslab import CompositionSpec, NodeSpec, TaskSpec, profile, resolve

target = resolve(profile(CompositionSpec(
    node=NodeSpec("falandays"),
    task=TaskSpec("tracking", options={"randomize_start": False}),
    count=32,
))).targets[0]
print(target.horizon, target.n_inputs, target.n_effectors)
```

Expect `2000 62 2`. Disabling random starts sets observer angle π/2, stimulus
angle zero and direction +1 unless explicitly overridden. The ordinary default
randomises the initial angles and direction.

## Read the native outcome

`track_score` is the mean cosine of angular error during the scored interval.
It ranges from −1 to 1: perfect alignment is 1, perpendicular alignment is zero
and opposite alignment is −1. The minimum scored horizon is 2,000 ticks.
Warm-up may precede scoring and still changes the world and adapting reservoir.

The random-action reference samples a uniform signed movement command each world
tick. Matching empirical calibration is required for a null-adjusted score.
The upper bound 1 is a mathematical bound, not a measured oracle.

A short diagnostic trajectory can show motion and interface behaviour. It cannot
establish ordinary task performance. Continue with
