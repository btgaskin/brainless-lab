title: Delayed XOR

## What relation is scored?

`delayed_xor` presents two independently chosen binary cues in separate receptor
banks. The correct response is left when the cues are equal and right when they
differ. This binary relation motivates the XOR name; the response coding is explicit.

The five receptors are first-left, first-right, second-left, second-right and go.
Two effectors vote during response only, with ties selecting left.
There is one neural frame per world tick.

```text
first cue (8) → gap (32) → second cue (8) → go / response (8)
```

```python
from brainlesslab import TaskSpec
from brainlesslab.tasks import definition, resolve

task = TaskSpec("delayed_xor", options={"gap": 32})
print(resolve(task))
print(definition(task))
```

The resolved options include transient cues and eight response ticks.
The definition reports five inputs, two effectors and horizon 56.
Four presets use gaps 0, 8, 32 and 128.

## Exclude simpler solutions

`cue_mode="transient"` removes each cue after its window.
`"persistent"` leaves the first bank visible after the first cue and the second bank
visible after the second cue. Both are then available during response, allowing a
memoryless relation computation. `"hidden"` suppresses both banks while retaining
the episode's random choices and timing.

The observation-based oracle retains both cues. Last-cue and constant policies
cannot in general recover their relation from a transient second cue alone.
Pair the visibility conditions with declared world identities.

`probe_accuracy` gives one binary result per full trial. Chance is 0.5, the
upper bound is 1 and warm-up is zero. A successful delayed relation task does not
establish general symbolic reasoning.

Continue with [decoding](/benchmarks/probes/decoding/) to separate recoverable cue
information from native response success.
