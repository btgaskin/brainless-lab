---
title: Recall duration and interference
description: Retain a binary cue through blank intervals and competing signals.
---

Recall probes ask how long a controller retains a cue and how competing input affects
that retention. `:delayed_cue` keeps its existing behaviour. The new
`:recall_interference` task inserts distractors on the same two cue channels.

```text
cue (8) → delay (0, 8, 32, 128 or 256) → go and response (8)
                  ↳ interference: one or three distractors, four frames each
```

The receptors are left cue, right cue and go. Outputs choose left or right. Only the
response window contributes to `recall_accuracy` on delayed cue or `probe_accuracy`
on interference. Both use raw and normalised accuracy in [0, 1], with chance 0.5.

```julia
using BrainlessLab
cell = only(c for c in capacity_probe_presets()
    if c.id == :recall_interference__delay_32_distractors_3)
target = EvaluationTarget(cell.id,
    CompositionSpec(cell.id, :null_random, cell.task; n_nodes=8, task_options=cell.task_options),
    EvaluationSpec(horizon=cell.horizon, root_seed=1610200))
task_outcome(only(evaluate(target).trials).simulation)
```

Distractors require at least 32 delay frames and blank spacing between events.
`distractor_mode` accepts `:random`, `:matched`, `:opposite` and `:absent`.
The seeded random distractor sequence stays fixed when visibility changes.
`cue_mode` accepts `:transient`, `:persistent` and `:hidden`. Hidden-cue controls
should use random or absent distractors: matched or opposite distractors reveal the cue.

An observation-only `probe_control(:recall_interference)` remembers the initial cue and
ignores later distractors. It should answer every completed trial correctly. A memoryless
controller succeeds when the cue remains visible through response. Hidden cue and constant
response establish the balanced-label chance reference. A short successful delay alone
does not establish long-duration retention or resistance to interference.

Use the [decoding diagnostic](/benchmarks/probes/decoding/) to locate recoverable cue
information, then compare the declared delay and distractor cells in the
[planned study](/benchmarks/probes/study/). Interference has not entered the core benchmark.
