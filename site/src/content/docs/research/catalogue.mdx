This catalogue reads `research/catalogue.json`. Each accepted contribution links its protocol,
role-labelled runs, and retained configuration files.

An accepted entry records a human maintainer decision. A `pre-pipeline` entry identifies
compatibility material that predates the contribution process.

<AcceptedRunCatalogue />

## Catalogue boundary

This page does not combine outcomes across runs or tasks. It does not rank contributions or draw
parameter-performance plots.

The contributor submission and maintainer replay are linked roles in one contribution. They are
not independent evidence. Read [Research records](/research/) for the acceptance process and
publication limits.
